OUR PURPOSE · SDG 6

Better information.
More mindful choices.

AQUACHECK is a student-led campus information project inspired by Sustainable Development Goal 6: Clean Water and Sanitation.

Our aim is to make water-point information easier to find and understand, while encouraging awareness and responsible water use.

✓ Location-based information✓ Clear report-status communication✓ Scope to expand campus monitoring
i
Important information about reports

This website is an information directory. Water-quality results will be displayed as actual findings only when authorised, documented reports have been received and checked. A missing report does not indicate that water is safe or unsafe.
